## 1. Prologue

It was way too long since I took an overseas trip and I hit on the idea to travel to New Zealand.  
I visited it there once in 2015 when I stayed in Australia for a Working Holiday and the wonderful experience vividly remained in me.  
The memory kicked my ass, so I chose to fly to New Zealand.

Do not ask the stupid question, "Why do you travel by yourself?" "Don't you feel lonely".  
The reasons I prefer to travel by myself are because I am happy to

- Follow the direction I myself choose
  - In a group trip, it is not allowed to choose 'left' if the others 'right'
- Maximise the chance to know someone new and broaden my worldview
- Confirm I can deal with unexpected matters by myself with help by someone local

## 2. Queentown

It has been 8 years since I paid the last visit in 2015 when I experienced a Working Holiday and dropped by it there.

On Day 1, I took a walk around the city and it rang a bell in me.  
The scenery of the town was as beautiful as that I last saw.  

On Day 2, I did the sight of Milford Sound for the second time, where it was rainy and cold till the early afternoon, but it cleared away and created a picturesque contrast between the white waterfalls and the blue sky.
It was pretty wonderful.

Have a look at [Photos taken in Queenstown](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipM4B4n4qvs5DIIQmMRpDJNhSdTovX41ObV2g0LSgeCUX2e-7JHiFc0jwNWrdHHKVg?key=ZjVDcVRjYlNiY1h6NWhCRUs2a1N1SWhlZ0lUN253).

## 3. Queenstown → Mount Cook

On Day 3, I was supposed to jump on a city bus at 07:00 in the morning, but I overslept and missed it.  
I negotiated with the reception and asked him to catch the kindest taxi driver who would bring me to Mount Cook over 250 km and luckily found one.  
It costed me another 500 NZD, but the driver was so kind and friendly that we eagerly talked about each other's work, religion, romance and families.  
Thanks to him, the 3-hour was rather fruitful than boring and it was worth another 500 NZD.

Have a look at [Photos taken on the way to Mount Cook](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipNxG6zvR-DV74c5H8Dx0GwTlHzAv_EgMpf1WQQHPPYQ5zQ1S6IF-KOYik6dheWgzQ?key=eUZqdm1pQWlrcjlVOEZ2LVo2S0FXUjlOWV9ITGJ3).

## 4. Mount Cook

On Day 3, I managed to arrive there and get back to the schedule.  
Since I rescheduled a tour I was supposed to join to the next day, I spent the rest of the day tracking a path, Kea Point.  
It took 2 hours to go to and from it.  
At night, I had dinner at a restaurant at the Hermitage Hotel because there were few options for a meal.  
It costed me as many as more than 150 NZD.

On Day 4, I got acquainted with an American tourist who was also a web/AI engineer.  
He was so gentle and friendly that we had a chat about each other's experiences of mount climbing.  
He started it 5 years ago and climbed a number of mountains in contrast to my poor experience.  
He had visited Japan a couple of times and was looking forward to giving it a try to Mount Fuji.  
After I said good-by to him, I joined in the tour rescheduled to this morning, Glacier Explorers.  
There was a world I had never seen and of which scenery was definitely the most crystal.  
The tour guide logically explained the mechanism of how the glacier was moulded and shaped, so I still remember some.

To see is to believe, so straight have a look at [Photos took in Mount Cook](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipOStv-MHW7JpvXbrL7MVg12-2ZKayGByMXRV6_zzZY0Pmei-BjXOO_TnG1Fiscjww?key=dlh2MTJPLTlFUGVNeFFoSmZaUXluaHI1QXgwZXp3).

## 5. Mount Cook → Lake Tekapo

A Japanese driver whose last name was Bansaka san brought me to Lake Tekapo with a Dutch couple other than me.  
We dropped by a lavender field, where I and the couple had ice cream over a chat.

Have a look at a few [Photos taken on the way to Lake Tekapo](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipO45rennKagzFFUKBJZFIBK90nkgXdMtuNh20nhvi0uvEHH8py5uDOllP8OO5FNzA?key=UWw2dFV6Vlg4NWVNZGRhaWc5OUZRS0syUHZ3T3BR).

## 6. Lake Tekapo

After I took some break after arrival, I took a stroll around the accommodation.  
There were loads of chatty and loud Chinese tourists I was annoyed with, but I was happy with the beautiful lake view, the famous church and the status of the dog.  
From 23:00 - 25:00, I joined a stargazing tour.  
Every time I watch constellations, I cannot recognise the shape even with explanation by the guide, but it was simply heart-cleansing to observe such beautiful stars and galaxies.  
The active duty observatory is remotely controlled by ISS(International Space Station) in the U.S.  
Unfortunately, I had no camera with high definition enough to take them.

Have a look at [Photos taken in Lake Tekapo](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipMRumB2oEUMsSZI21YdQxXU8h-I4j-I0vh_guLgsHVoihCc_7MO7luY6mjlEN29AQ?key=UjNIZW9UdkJCT0ZLbFVVUXBXaU50cGlERmNjYnlR).

## 7. Christchurch

On Day 5, I got up on time and did not miss the intercity bus, different from the mistake I made in Queenstown.  
What I was surprised at was that Christchurch was the biggest city in the South Island(the 2nd biggest city in New Zealand overall).  
I expected it there to allow me to take good photos, but I did not because it was boring.

On Day 6, I jumped on Tranz Apline to get to Arthurs Pass.  
The day excursion allowed me to trace various kinds of paths within my ability.  
The duration was only 5 hours, I could take only 3 courses.  
There was tranquil scenery under my nose.

Have a look at [Photos taken in Christchurch\(mainly in Arthurs Pass\)](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipP5mVAQlpy7zlmYDOY5UYezQmgqQXUpMdkqSYZIDkxP1UAntd_U-s7VkCpv8WDb3w?key=ZjJaSXJYNjZMVURvUTNDSEJzNmdMTHlEWEdlS21n).

I saw lots of nice people, enjoyed picturesque sceneries and communicated in English(a lot more comfortable than in Japanese).
